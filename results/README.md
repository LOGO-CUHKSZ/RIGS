# Recorded Top30 / 65M / RDKit5 results

These are the saved seed-42 results for the supplied model, not results produced while preparing this release. `reference_result.json` stores exact recorded arm and per-axis rates in percent. The CSVs retain the paper's displayed precision.

`common_top100_absolute_arms.csv` reports returned-list and validity rates in percent. `common_top100_paired_effects.csv` reports effects and confidence intervals in percentage points. `common_top100_reference_budgets.csv` reports hit/validity/duplicate rates in percent, pooled recovery as a count, and unique-valid as a mean count. All files are restricted to Top30 / 65M.
